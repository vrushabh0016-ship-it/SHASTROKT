The Founder

Shastri Shri Harshad Joshi

A Sanskrit scholar from Mysore, Karnataka, Shastri Ji came to Mumbai in 1990 and founded Shastrokt on his own.

His work has always centred on two energies: Prāṇic Ūrjā, the life force that moves through a building, and Chitta Ūrjā, the energy of the mind of those who live and work in it. When the two are in balance, a home feels peaceful and a business grows steadily.

His two sons assisted him for many years and now carry the Shastrokt legacy forward, bringing the same scriptural method to a new generation of families and enterprises.
